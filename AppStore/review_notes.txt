Shinkeisuijaku - Notes for App Review
A memory (concentration) card game for young children in the Kids Category (age band: 5 and under). The UI is Japanese only; labels are quoted as shown, with English in [square brackets].

1. SCREEN RECORDING
The attached recording from a physical iPhone (iPhone 13) starts from the Home Screen and shows: the first game starting without the home screen, clearing it, the home screen, the stamp book, the locked Normal and Hard, the parental gate, the price, the sandbox purchase, the Normal game starting right after it, and the purchased state.
No account, login, or user-generated content. One In-App Purchase (section 3).

2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
An ad-free memory game for children aged 5 and under. Children turn over cards to find pairs in two courses: "どうぶつ" [Animals] (the same picture) and "ひらがな" [Hiragana] (a hiragana letter and a picture).
It does not pressure children: no timer, move or mistake count, score, or ranking. A wrong pair just turns back over, with no "X" mark or warning sound. The app has no sound or voice.

3. HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
First launch: until the first game is cleared, the app skips the home screen and starts a 6-pair "どうぶつ" [Animals] game. After that, it starts from the home screen. (To reach home without clearing, tap "やめる" [Quit], then "やめる" [Quit] in the alert.)
"ヒント" [Hint] shows all cards for about a second. Clearing a game gives a stamp; "スタンプちょう" [Stamp book] on home shows them.
"やさしい" [Easy] (6 pairs) is free. "ふつう" [Normal] (8 pairs) and "むずかしい" [Hard] (10 pairs) need the In-App Purchase jp.shilokuma.Shinkeisuijaku.pass (Non-Consumable, "ぜんぶ遊べるパス" [All-access pass]). To test it, either:
  a. Home > "おうちの方へ" [For parents] at the top right > parental gate > "購入する" [Buy] or "購入を復元" [Restore Purchases].
  b. Home > "どうぶつ" [Animals] under "ほかの あそびかた" [Other ways to play] > the locked "ふつう" [Normal] > "おうちの方に あけてもらってね" [Ask a grown-up to unlock it] > "おうちの方へ" [For parents] > parental gate > "購入する" [Buy]. After the purchase, that game starts.
After the purchase, "ぜんぶ遊べるパスは購入済みです" [The pass is purchased] replaces the buy and restore buttons.
Parental gate: enter the sum of a 2-digit and a 1-digit number (e.g. "12 ＋ 7 ＝ ？", answer 19) with the number pad and tap "決定" [OK]. A wrong answer changes the question. The price, purchase, restore, and external links appear only after the gate.

4. KIDS CATEGORY (GUIDELINE 1.3)
- Third-party analytics: none. No analytics, crash reporting, tracking, or other third-party SDKs; only Apple's frameworks.
- Third-party advertising: none.
- Data shared with third parties: none. The app makes no network requests of its own and works offline. It uses only Apple's StoreKit for the price, purchase, and restore.
- Other data collection: none. No personal information or device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV). Only the stamps, the last course and difficulty, whether the first game was cleared, and a cached purchase flag are stored on the device (UserDefaults); they never leave it. Privacy manifest: no tracking, no collected data. App Privacy: "Data Not Collected".
- External links: only the privacy policy and a contact form (Google Forms) on the "おうちの方へ" [For parents] screen, after the parental gate. They open in Safari; there is no in-app browser.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. Distributed in Japan only, as the app is Japanese only.
